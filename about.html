About Sally's Smile

A community-based charity in Ghana dedicated to improving the lives of vulnerable children and families.

Who We Are

A foundation built on compassion, dignity, and action.

We believe every child deserves a healthy smile, access to learning, and the opportunity to grow in a safe and supportive environment. Our outreach blends education, health, environmental care, and social empowerment to bring lasting positive change.

Our Vision

A society where every child and vulnerable person lives with dignity, good health, and equal opportunity.

Our Mission

Support disadvantaged communities through education, healthcare outreach, environmental protection, and humanitarian assistance.

Core Objectives

The promises guiding our daily work.

Educational Support

Provide school materials and educational support for underprivileged children.

Health & Hygiene

Promote good health and hygiene through community outreach programs.

Environmental Protection

Protect the environment through clean-up campaigns and tree planting.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Empower women and youth with skills training and livelihood opportunities.

Support for Orphans

Support orphans and vulnerable children with basic needs.

Target Group

Orphans, widows, the blind, people living with leprosy, people with limb differences, the deaf, street children, aged women, street hawkers, and youth.

Funding

Activities are financed from profits made from Smile Naturals Skincare Products, contributions from members, and support from well-wishers.

The Smile Team

A committed team of 23 active members.

The foundation is led by the Founder, two Board Directors, and executive members. Every member is fully active in the discharge of tasks assigned to them.

Community-led: Local knowledge shapes every decision.
Action-driven: Outreach programs translate compassion into impact.
