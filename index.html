About Us

A society where everyone lives with dignity and opportunity.

We support disadvantaged communities through education, healthcare outreach, environmental protection, and humanitarian assistance. Every project brings real change to lives and strengthens community resilience.

Vision: A society where every child and vulnerable person lives with dignity, good health, and equal opportunity.
Mission: Support disadvantaged communities through education, healthcare outreach, environmental protection, and humanitarian assistance.
Target Group

We prioritize orphans, widows, the blind, people living with leprosy, people with limb differences, the deaf, street children, aged women, street hawkers, and youth.

Funding: Programs are financed through Smile Naturals Skincare product profits, member contributions, and well-wishers.
Team: 23 members, including the Founder, two Board Directors, and executive members who are fully active.

Key Activities

Programs that translate compassion into action.

From education to environmental care, our activities are designed to bring lasting change.

School Outreach: Distribution of books, uniforms, and learning materials.
Health & Hygiene Campaigns: Community education on sanitation and disease prevention.
Environmental Projects: Tree planting, waste management awareness, and clean communities.
Community Support: Food donations and support for vulnerable families.
Youth Empowerment: Skills training and mentorship for youth and women groups.
Recent Highlights

Milestones from the Smile Journey.

Visited Anfaani Children's Home in Vittin to donate essential items and share support.

2022

Donated to St. Monica's Catholic Basic School, held visits to Nazareth Home of God's Children, and organized disability group support in Tamale.

2023

Provided cooked food, street giveaways, and outreach to Zuo community and vulnerable street hawkers.

2025

Delivered clothing, food, detergents, and baby items to widows and families in the Lingbinsi community.
