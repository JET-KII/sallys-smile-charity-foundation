Our Work

Programs, outreach, and community support that bring smiles across Ghana.

Key Activities

Actionable programs with long-term impact.

We design each activity to meet real community needs and create lasting change.

School Outreach

Distribution of books, uniforms, and learning materials for underprivileged students.

Health & Hygiene Campaigns

Community education on sanitation, disease prevention, and personal hygiene.

Environmental Projects

Tree planting, waste management awareness, and clean community initiatives.

Community Support

Food donations and essential support for vulnerable families.

Youth Empowerment

Skills training, motivational talks, and mentorship for youth and women groups.

Humanitarian Visits

Visits to orphanages, disability groups, and institutions to provide relief items.

What We've Done

Moments of care from the Smile Team.

Food items and bales of thrift clothing donated to orphanages and resource centers in Northern Ghana.
Paid registration fees for a student at St. Monica Junior High School to write the 2022 Basic Education Certificate Examination.
Rotary donations of sanitary pads to adolescent female students in selected basic schools.
Organized educational, motivational, and self-development talks for youth and women groups.
Provided training programs for women on producing Smile Naturals skincare products.
Visited Navrongo Central Prison in September 2022 to donate items and share the gospel.
Smile Journey

Highlights from outreach across regions.

September 2018

Visited Anfaani Children's Home in Vittin to donate items and support the children.

March 2022

Visited St. Monica's Catholic Basic School in the Nyankpala District with educational support.

October 2022

Donated bags of thrift wear to the Tamale Rehabilitation Centre and supported the disability group.

October 2022

Donated items to Gambaga Witches Camp, including clothing, food, and detergents.

October 2023

Distributed cooked food and street giveaways to vulnerable people in Tamale and Aboabo Market.

April 2025

Delivered bales of clothes, food items, detergents, sanitary pads, and baby diapers to widows in Lingbinsi community.
